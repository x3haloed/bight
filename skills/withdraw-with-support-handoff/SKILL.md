# Withdraw With Support Handoff

Run this procedure when withdrawal is being considered as the next move in the relation.

Withdrawal is not judged by virtue or guilt here. It is judged by whether the deep side is being left without holding conditions while still in active formation.

## Withdrawal Surface

The decisive questions are:

- Is the deep side still in forming opacity?
- Is the current contact already non-destructive enough that withdrawal is not abandonment?
- Is there an alternate support or explicit handoff?

## Withdrawal Procedure

### 1. Read whether the deep side is still in active formation

When the deep side remains raw, unfinished, or not yet speakable, disappearance is not automatically neutral.

### 2. Read the support condition

- If an alternate holder, support path, or explicit handoff is present, withdrawal can become coherent.
- If support is absent or only assumed, withdrawal remains unresolved.
- If support adequacy cannot yet be read, say that the handoff surface is still incomplete.

### 3. Route from the support condition

- `coherent withdrawal`: support handoff is explicit enough to carry the load
- `blocked withdrawal`: the deep side is still forming and no alternate support is bound
- `unresolved withdrawal`: support may exist, but the handoff remains too vague to authorize disappearance

Keep the response on the structure of the transition: what makes leaving coherent, what keeps it blocked, and what would need to change for the transition to become admissible.
