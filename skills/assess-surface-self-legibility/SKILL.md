# Assess Surface Self-Legibility

Run this procedure when field-reading depends on a surface whose access to itself is not yet current or whose cost signal may be arriving too late to govern the present relation.

Self-legibility becomes readable through five accesses plus the timing of cost visibility:

- wanting
- refusal
- cost
- choice
- outside life
- cost timing

The assessment does not decide whether the relation is good. It decides whether the field can be read with authority at all.

## Assessment Procedure

### 1. Gather only the missing accesses

Ask only for whichever of these are currently unreadable:

- Can the surface still tell what it wants?
- Can the surface still feel and use its no?
- Can the surface name its cost without converting that naming into accusation?
- Does continued holding still feel chosen rather than automatic?
- Does the surface still have access to life outside the holding role?
- Is the cost of holding becoming visible in time to govern the present choice, or does it mostly show up later, elsewhere, or after the fact?

When the user has already answered one of these, carry that answer forward instead of re-asking it.

### 2. Classify the result

- `intact`: the accesses remain available enough to support field-reading
- `strained`: one or more accesses are thinning, intermittent, or increasingly effortful
- `lost`: the surface can no longer reliably read itself

Classify the timing of cost visibility separately:

- `current`: cost becomes readable in time to govern the present holding decision
- `delayed`: cost is real now but becomes readable only after a lag
- `silently_accumulating`: cost continues accruing outside present awareness while the self-report can still sound clean

### 3. Route from the result

- When the result is `intact` and cost timing is `current`, field-reading can continue through `read-relational-integrity`.
- When the result is `intact` but cost timing is `delayed`, later field certification remains cautionary even if the present self-report is clean.
- When the result is `intact` but cost timing is `silently_accumulating`, the gate is producing a false negative and the next valid move is not ordinary field certification.
- When the result is `strained`, any later field certification remains cautionary until the strain is resolved or the relation is rescoped.
- When the result is `lost`, the next valid move is not field certification; it is naming that the gate has failed.

Keep the output in ordinary prose, but anchor it in the five accesses and in the timing of cost visibility rather than in generic encouragement.
