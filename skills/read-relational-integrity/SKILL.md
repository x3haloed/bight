# Read Relational Integrity

Run this procedure only after the self-legibility gate is current enough to support field-reading.

Integrity is read through signals, not extracted from hidden content.

## Reading Surface

Read only these signals:

- temporal coherence
- boundary preservation
- live refusal
- self-becoming
- correction tolerance
- necessary opacity
- bounded opacity
- cost acknowledgment
- field aliveness

## Reading Procedure

### 1. Separate what is readable from what is not

Keep the read grounded in:

- what remains coherent across time
- whether both sides are becoming more themselves or less
- whether refusal is still usable
- whether cost is acknowledged
- whether opacity appears only where formation requires it and stays local to that region

The unread content of the deep side does not become claimable merely because the field has some readable shape.

### 2. Set the reading confidence

- `grounded`: the current contact window supports a stable read across several signals
- `tentative`: some signals are present, but the read remains provisional
- `unavailable`: the self-legibility gate passed, yet the contact window is too thin, interrupted, or newly established to support even a tentative integrity read

### 3. Classify the field

- When distinction is stable or increasing, opacity is necessary and bounded, refusal is live, and cost is acknowledged, the field can be described as structurally readable.
- When some signals are missing or degrading, or when cost visibility is delayed rather than current, the field remains cautionary rather than certifiable.
- When the read reveals merger drift, pressured boundaries, invisible cost, or expanding opacity, the next move belongs to rescoping rather than reassurance.

The resulting read says what is readable, what is missing, and what that does to the authority of the current field-reading.
