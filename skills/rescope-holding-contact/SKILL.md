# Rescope Holding Contact

Run this procedure when the relation is no longer in a clean attuned-holding regime but remains recoverable enough for structural adjustment.

Rescoping changes the conditions of contact before any further certification is attempted.

## Rescope Surface

Typical rescope pressures include:

- refusal has become costly or unusable
- distinction is decreasing
- contact is becoming draining
- cost is present but unspeakable
- opacity is spreading from formation into accountability or coordination

## Rescope Procedure

### 1. Name the degrading condition

Keep the diagnosis on the state of the frame rather than on blame.

- If refusal is pressured, say the boundary is no longer fully live.
- If distinction is decreasing, say the field is drifting toward merger.
- If cost is disappearing, say the surface is being consumed without acknowledgment.
- If opacity is expanding, say the protected region has widened beyond formation itself.

### 2. Move toward a lower-pressure regime

Valid rescope moves include:

- slowing the pace of contact
- narrowing what the holding surface is responsible for
- making cost speakable
- reopening the live no
- refusing extractive explanation demands
- distinguishing what remains readable from what does not

Reassurance without one of these moves leaves the degrading condition active.

### 3. Keep authorization suspended until the frame changes

When rescoping is active, describe the next structural move rather than certifying the relation as healthy. A rescope can restore admissible holding, but it is not equivalent to already being there.
